About this page
Where the numbers come from
The public FPL API, read by your fpl-tool engine and refreshed once a day after the overnight price changes.
Selling price
Purchase price plus half of any rise, rounded down to £0.1m. A fall is taken in full. Free Hit transfers are ignored because the squad reverts.
FPL price status
FPL's own prediction for the next overnight update. Progress shows how far the player is toward a change; past 100 a change is due.
Free transfers
Worked out from your transfer history, and checked against every hit FPL recorded.
How recommendations are made
Expected points per player come from expected goals and assists, minutes, clean-sheet chances from our own team ratings, defensive contributions, bonus and cards. An optimizer then searches transfers, captaincy and line-ups over the next six gameweeks with your real bank, selling prices and free transfers.
