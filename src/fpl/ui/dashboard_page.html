About this page
Where the numbers come from
The public FPL API, read by your fpl-tool engine and refreshed once a day after the overnight price changes.
Selling price
Purchase price plus half of any rise, rounded down to £0.1m. A fall is taken in full. Free Hit transfers are ignored because the squad reverts.

FPL's own prediction for the next overnight update. Progress shows how far the player is toward a change; past 100 a change is due.
